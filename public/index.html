Everyone starts from zero in Week 2

The baseline resets to wherever the frontier stands at the end of Week 1, and only improvements pushed from that point count toward the Weeks 2 and 3 pool and the raffle. Week 1 improvements do not carry forward: early entrants can take very large gains simply by being first, and carrying those forward would make the later rounds unwinnable for anyone who joins afterwards.
